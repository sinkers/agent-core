# AS 4000 clause map (synthesis from public commentary, not the standard's text)

Sources: law firm and practitioner commentary (Turtons, MinterEllison Construction Law Made Easy, HWL Ebsworth,
CBP, McCullough, Corrs, Doyles), checked for numbering against publicly posted amended AS 4000-1997 contracts.
The exact words are in the purchased standard or the executed contract: read those before
relying on any of this for a decision.

## AS 4000-1997 top-level clauses

1 Interpretation · 2 Nature of contract · 3 Provisional sums · 4 Separable portions · 5 Security · 6 Evidence of
contract · 7 Service of notices · 8 Contract documents · 9 Assignment and subcontracting · 10 Intellectual
property · 11 Legislative requirements · 12 Protection of people and property · 13 Urgent protection · 14 Care of
the work · 15 Damage to persons and property · 16 Insurance of the works · 17 Public liability insurance ·
18 Insurance of employees · 19 Inspection of insurance policies · 20 Superintendent · 21 Superintendent's
representative · 22 Contractor's representative · 23 Contractor's employees and subcontractors · 24 Site ·
25 Latent conditions · 26 Setting out · 27 Cleaning up · 28 Materials, labour and plant · 29 Quality ·
30 Examination and testing · 31 Working hours · 32 Programming · 33 Suspension · 34 Time and progress ·
35 Defects liability · 36 Variations · 37 Payment · 38 Payment of workers and subcontractors · 39 Default or
insolvency · 40 Termination by frustration · 41 Notification of claims · 42 Dispute resolution · 43 Waiver

Common confusions: cl 17 is public liability insurance (not latent conditions — that's cl 25 only); cl 21 is the
superintendent's representative; cl 33 is suspension (programming is cl 32); cl 35 is defects liability (time and
LDs are in cl 34). AS 2124 numbering differs: latent conditions cl 12, superintendent cl 23, EOT cl 35.5, LDs
cl 35.6, variations cl 40, payment cl 42, time-barred claims cl 46, disputes cl 47.

## Key sub-clauses (1997, unamended as commonly described)

- **3 Provisional sums**: the superintendent directs the work; the contract sum is adjusted by the difference,
  with the Annexure margin on excess.
- **5 Security**: 5.1 provision, 5.2 recourse, 5.3 change, 5.4 reduction and release (reduced at PC, released after
  the final certificate per the Annexure), 5.5 trusts and interest.
- **8 Contract documents**: 8.1 discrepancies (superintendent directs interpretation), 8.2 principal-supplied,
  8.3 contractor-supplied, 8.7 records.
- **9 Assignment and subcontracting**: consent to assign; superintendent's approval to subcontract; 9.3 selected
  subcontract work.
- **20 Superintendent**: principal ensures there is always one; acts reasonably and in good faith as assessor and
  certifier; directions oral but confirmed in writing; principal may replace the superintendent with notice.
- **25 Latent conditions**: 25.1 scope (materially different from what a competent contractor should have
  anticipated from information, enquiries and inspection; weather excluded); 25.2 prompt notice, before disturbing
  where possible; 25.3 deemed variation valued under 36.4, excluding costs more than 28 days before notice.
- **29 Quality**: 29.3 defective work (correct or remove); 29.4 acceptance of defective work; 29.6 warranties.
- **30 Examination and testing**: 30.1 tests; 30.2 covering up (notice before covering); 30.7 costs.
- **32 Programming**: superintendent may direct a programme and the order/timing of work; contractor not to depart
  without reasonable cause; cost of complying with a programming direction not caused by the contractor is adjusted.
- **33 Suspension**: 33.1 superintendent's suspension; 33.2 contractor's suspension; 33.3 recommencement; 33.4 cost.
- **34 Time and progress**: 34.1 progress; 34.2 notice of delay (prompt, to superintendent and principal); 34.3 EOT
  claim within 28 days; 34.4 assessment (mitigation; concurrent causes apportioned); 34.5 superintendent's decision
  within 28 days or deemed granted, plus a unilateral power to extend time; 34.6 practical completion (14 days'
  notice of anticipated PC; certify or reasons within 14 days); 34.7 liquidated damages (superintendent certifies);
  34.8 bonus for early PC (often deleted); 34.9 delay damages for compensable causes.
- **35 Defects liability**: DLP from 4 pm on the date of PC (length in Annexure, often 12 months); rectification
  directions; principal may rectify at contractor's cost; separate DLP for rectified work.
- **36 Variations**: 36.1 written direction (before PC); 36.2 proposed variations; 36.3 for contractor's
  convenience; 36.4 valuation (agreed → contract rates → bill/schedule rates as reasonable → reasonable rates;
  deductions include profit, not overheads).
- **37 Payment**: 37.1 progress claims at Annexure times; 37.2 progress certificate within 14 days (claim deemed the
  certificate if none), payment within 7 days of certificate and tax invoice; 37.3 unfixed plant and materials
  (if in Annexure, with security, insurance, title, labelling); 37.4 final payment claim within 28 days after the
  DLP, final certificate within 14 days, conclusive except fraud, latent defects, computational errors and disputes
  notified within 7 days; 37.5 interest (default 18 % simple unless the Annexure says otherwise).
- **38 Payment of workers and subcontractors**: statutory declaration; principal may withhold or pay subcontractors
  directly.
- **39 Default or insolvency**: 39.2 substantial breaches; 39.3 show cause notice (at least 7 clear days);
  39.4 take work out of hands or terminate; 39.7-39.10 principal's default; 39.11 insolvency.
- **41 Notification of claims**: 41.1 prescribed notice within 28 days; 41.2 failure to notify gives damages (not a
  bar) in the unamended form — often amended into a bar; 41.3 particulars within 28 days, then assessment.
- **42 Dispute resolution**: 42.1 notice of dispute; 42.2 conference within 14 days; 42.3 arbitration or litigation
  if unresolved within 28 days of the notice; 42.4 urgent relief always available.

## AS 4000:2025 — what changed (commentary as at Oct 2026)

- Risk allocation unchanged (Abrahamson principles).
- All definitions in cl 1; Formal Instrument of Agreement included; "must" replaces "shall"; many fixed periods
  become "as soon as practicable"; email service of notices recognised.
- New: a GST clause dealing with tax invoices and RCTIs; appointment of the Principal Contractor under WHS law
  (cl 12.2); PPSA clause (cl 44); deed of novation (Annexure Part D).
- Latent conditions notice "as soon as practicable".
- Variations effective only if directed in writing before PC; deemed-variation threshold for quantity differences
  raised from $400 to $1,000.
- PC requires legislative requirements to be satisfied, and can occur before the certificate.
- Delay damages measure defined more tightly.
- Disputes (cl 42): negotiation first (42.3), then Annexure options: mediation (42.5), expert determination (42.6),
  mediation then expert determination (42.7), dispute avoidance board (42.8); final resolution (42.4) arbitration
  or litigation; default negotiation then litigation.
- Commentators disagree on some new clause numbers (e.g. EOT at cl 34 or 35): check the 2025 text.
- AS 4902, AS 4901, AS 4903 and HB 140 (administration manual) are expected to be revised to align; as at October
  2026 the 2000-era versions remain current.

## AS 4902-2000 differences from AS 4000

Contractor designs (cl 2); design warranty of fitness for the **stated** purpose in the Principal's Project
Requirements (principals often amend to "intended" — fitness for purpose is generally not covered by professional
indemnity insurance); design documents submitted to the superintendent but review doesn't shift design risk;
consultant novation under cl 9 using the Annexure Part D deed, with the consultants listed in Annexure Part A.
Delivery routes: D&C, design development and construct, design novate and construct.
